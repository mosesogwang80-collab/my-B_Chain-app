About Us

B_Chain is a decentralized waste management coordination platform designed to bridge the gap between residents (Reporters) and private waste collectors. By digitizing the request and payment flow, we ensure Nakawa stays clean through efficient, transparent service delivery.

Our Aim

Our primary objective is to eliminate illegal dumping and delayed waste collection. We aim to provide real-time tracking of waste hotspots, ensure fair pricing for residents, and guarantee timely payouts for collection companies.

Our Vision

To be the leading smart-city solution for environmental sanitation in Uganda, fostering a culture of cleanliness and sustainability through innovative technology and community coordination.

Our Location & Inventors

Developed by the dedicated B_Chain Engineering Team, this innovative project was conceived and operates from its central hub at Nakawa UICT in Uganda. Our mission is driven by a passion for environmental sustainability and urban technological innovation.

Terms, Conditions & Policies

1. User Responsibility: Reporters must ensure that waste is properly sorted and accessible at the specified location upon the collector's arrival.

2. Payment Policy: Payments are processed via Mobile Money or Cash. Mobile Money payments are held until task completion is verified to ensure collector accountability.

3. Privacy: We protect user data. Contact information is only shared between a Reporter and their specifically allocated Collector to facilitate service.

4. System Maintenance: A 2% levy is applied to every transaction to facilitate system maintenance and operational costs.

5. Deactivation: The system administrator reserves the right to deactivate any account (Reporter or Collector) found violating standard environmental laws or system protocols.

🛡️ Safety & Security First

Security Tips

Protect Passwords: Never share your login or transaction passwords. Use strong, unique combinations.

Verify Receipts: Always request and confirm a company receipt when paying cash for services.

Payment Advice

Confirm Amount: Drivers/Collectors, always confirm the full payment amount before completing any transaction.

Official Requests: All transactions must be done through the B_Chain system to be valid.
